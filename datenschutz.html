Hosting / server logs

The technical operation of this website is performed through GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). Each request may result in the processing of server log data, such as the IP address, the time of the request, and the page requested. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR: a legitimate interest in a proper and secure operation of the website. Log data are deleted when they are no longer required for this purpose. For further information on data processing, GitHub's privacy policy applies (https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy).

Data processing by the website

The website can be used without providing any personal data. No cookies are set and no analytics or tracking tools are embedded. The pages consist of our own HTML, CSS, and JavaScript; fonts and scripts are not loaded from external servers. There is no contact form. The language choice (EN/DE) is stored exclusively locally in your browser (localStorage) and is not transmitted to the website.
